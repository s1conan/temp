Our Story

Creativity at the heart of every celebration.

Saxbys Marquees is a luxury marquee and events company built on one belief — that the setting should feel as extraordinary as the moment it holds.

For over fifteen years we've partnered with couples, families and brands to transform fields, gardens and estates into unforgettable spaces. As market-leading suppliers, we handle everything from the first sketch to the final fairy light, so you can be fully present on the day that matters.

Our approach is personal and unhurried. We listen, we advise, and we obsess over the details others overlook — because true luxury is felt in the seams, the sightlines and the softness of the light.

  • Market-leading suppliers with an unrivalled network of craftspeople and stylists.
  • Creativity at its core — every marquee is styled individually, never off-the-shelf.
  • Years of industry experience delivering weddings, festivals and corporate events.

Stretch Tent
